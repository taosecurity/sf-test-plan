# Privacy

- sf-test-plan makes no network connections. The command-line tool reads the files you name and writes only the files it says it writes (the page, a results file, a marks file, a starter plan, and the log file when you ask for one).
- The page loads nothing from the internet: its style and script are inside the file. It keeps your marks and notes in your browser's local storage for that page, and nowhere else. Export writes them to a file on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
- Notes you type go into exported marks files and results files. Leave out anything you don't want to share before you send those files to someone.
